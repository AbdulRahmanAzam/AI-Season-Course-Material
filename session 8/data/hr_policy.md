# HR Policy Knowledge Base (Demo Data)

Each block is indexed separately. One chunk is intentionally outdated.

---

## POLICY-2024-001 | Remote Work (Current)

Effective January 2024. All full-time employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. At least 2 days must be spent in the office for team collaboration. Manager approval required for fully remote arrangements.

---

## POLICY-2024-002 | Remote Work — Engineering Track

Engineering staff on Level 2+ may work remotely up to **4 days per week** after completing their probation period. Junior engineers (Level 1) follow the standard 3-day remote limit in POLICY-2024-001.

---

## POLICY-2022-ARCHIVED | Remote Work (Superseded)

**ARCHIVED — DO NOT USE.** Old 2022 draft stated unlimited remote work. This policy was **never approved** and was replaced by POLICY-2024-001. Kept only for audit history.

---

## POLICY-2024-003 | Leave & PTO

Employees receive 20 days paid time off per year. Unused PTO rolls over up to 5 days into the next calendar year.

---

## POLICY-2024-004 | Expense Reimbursement

Home internet stipend: $50/month for employees on hybrid or remote schedules. Submit receipts via the finance portal within 30 days.
